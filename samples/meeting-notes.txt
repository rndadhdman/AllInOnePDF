SCOPE AND PHASES
----------------

The interface lands first: shell, convert workspace, reader, theme builder.
Then Markdown to PDF with full theme support, and PDF back to Markdown.

Further formats follow through the neutral document model so each one is a single importer or exporter.

RISKS

Fidelity of PDF to Markdown depends on layout analysis. Tables and multi-column pages are the hard cases.
Fonts: stick to bundled fonts for identical output on Windows and Linux.
